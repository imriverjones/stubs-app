Stash keeps the tickets that won't go in Apple Wallet: ferries, trains, gigs, theatre, activities and Airbnb check-ins. Share a PDF or screenshot to Stash and it files it by date, reminds you before, and puts the code on your lock screen on the day.

No account. No ads. Your tickets never leave your phone.
