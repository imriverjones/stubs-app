Privacy policy

Last updated 9 October 2026
The short version: Stash has no accounts and no servers. Your tickets, stays and photos stay on your iPhone. We don't collect, store, sell or share any of your data.

What Stash keeps, and where

Everything you add to Stash — tickets, PDFs, screenshots, check-in details, cover photos and settings — is saved only on your iPhone, inside the app. Codes and text are read on the phone itself using Apple's built-in tools. Nothing is uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What Stash asks to use

  • Photos — to add ticket screenshots you pick, to set cover or background photos, and (only if you turn it on) to spot new screenshots that contain a ticket code. Photos are checked on the phone and never uploaded.
  • Camera — to scan paper tickets. Scans are saved in the app only.
  • Notifications — to remind you before a ticket or check-in. Reminders are scheduled on the phone.
  • Motion — so holographic tickets shimmer as you tilt the phone. Motion data isn't stored.

When something leaves your phone

  • When you send it. If you share a ticket or stay with someone, it goes through the app you choose (Messages, WhatsApp, Mail…). That app's privacy policy applies.
  • Ticket links. If you open a booking link in Stash, the booking site loads as it would in Safari, and that site's privacy policy applies.
  • Add to Stash links. A company's "Add to Stash" link carries the ticket inside the link itself. Stash doesn't send it anywhere.
  • App updates. Stash checks Expo's servers for app updates. This sends technical details such as the app version, not your tickets.

No tracking

Stash has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and doesn't use your data to follow you across other apps or websites.

Children

Stash doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
