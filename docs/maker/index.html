Stash for business

Add to Stash button

The Apple Wallet button for companies who don't have one. Your customers tap it in their confirmation email, and the ticket is on their lock screen on the day, with a reminder and your meeting point. No app to build, no Wallet certificates.

1 · Ticket details

Use exactly what your scanners read (the text inside your current QR codes). One line per person; they'll swipe between them in Stash.

2 · Your button

Tap it to see what your customer sees.
Fill in a title, date and at least one ticket.

3 · Lots of bookings? (CSV)

Columns: title, date (YYYY-MM-DD), time, ref, code, seat, name. One row per ticket; rows with the same ref become one booking. Uses the company, type and “on the day” text above.

Why customers love it

  • Ticket on their lock screen on the day, and the QR code opens at full brightness.
  • A reminder 2 hours before, with your meeting point.
  • No more digging through emails at the gate: faster boarding, fewer no-shows.
  • No app to download first: without Stash, the link still shows their ticket and QR code.
